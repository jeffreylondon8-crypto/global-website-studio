Contact FAQs

Answers to common questions about contacting us

How quickly do you respond to inquiries? +

We typically respond to emails within 24 hours and phone calls during business hours. For urgent matters, WhatsApp messages often receive the fastest response.

Do you offer free consultations? +

Yes! We offer a free 30-minute consultation to discuss your project, understand your needs, and provide initial recommendations. There's no obligation to proceed after the consultation.

What information should I provide when contacting you? +

When reaching out, it's helpful to include: your business name, type of project, desired timeline, and budget range if you have one. The more details you provide, the better we can assist you.

Do you work with clients outside South Africa? +

Absolutely! We work with clients worldwide. We're experienced in remote collaboration and can accommodate different time zones and currencies.

What is your typical project timeline? +

Timelines vary based on project complexity. Simple websites can take 2-3 weeks, while complex e-commerce sites may take 4-8 weeks. We provide detailed timelines during our consultation.
