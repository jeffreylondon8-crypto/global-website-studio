> Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical website project take?

+

Timelines vary based on project complexity. A basic website typically takes 2-3 weeks, while larger e-commerce projects can take 4-8 weeks. I provide detailed timelines during our initial consultation.

What is your payment structure?

+

I require a 50% deposit to begin work, with the remaining 50% due upon project completion. For larger projects, I offer milestone-based payments.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

+

Yes! I offer monthly maintenance plans that include updates, security monitoring, backups, and technical support. These start at R500/month.

What technologies do you work with?

+

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (React, Vue), Node.js, Python, various CMS platforms (WordPress, Shopify), and database systems. I choose the best technology stack for each specific project.

Can I update my website myself?

+

Absolutely! I provide training and documentation so you can manage content yourself. For more complex updates, I'm available for support.
